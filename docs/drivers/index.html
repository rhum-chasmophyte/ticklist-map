What drives my sends

Each climbing day is compared with every other day at the same crag and time of year over 25 years. The conditions that set send days apart show what to wait for, and they differ by region.

The short version

Which conditions matter most

How often each condition alone picks the send day over a comparable day at the same crag and season, tested on years the model didn't see. 50% is a coin flip. Lines show the 90% range.

Sweet spots

Top: how likely a send is across each condition's usual range, relative to its best value (shaded band = within 80% of best). Bottom: where send days actually fell compared with all available days.

What matters where

Crags are grouped into climate regions by their 25-year pattern of temperature, humidity and rain. Each cell shows how often that condition picks the send day there; ↑ means send days ran higher than typical, ↓ lower. Grey cells aren't clearly different from chance. Pick a region for its details.
